Who We Are

One Partner.
Every Digital Need.

LARA.ai was founded by Latika Sharma with a single conviction: technology should solve real problems, not just demo well. We are a New Delhi-based technology company — registered as an MSME with the Government of India — that takes businesses from first website to full AI-powered operations.

We build in-house, end to end. Websites and mobile apps engineered for performance. Custom software with long-term support and maintenance. AI solutions built on Anthropic's Claude — not as a wrapper, but as core intelligence. And growth systems: Meta and Google advertising, market research, and WhatsApp Business API automation that keep customers coming back.

From political campaign technology to hospitality brands to news portals, we've shipped across industries — because good engineering doesn't care about verticals.

"We didn't start LARA to chase the AI hype. We started it because we saw what this technology could actually do — and realised that most businesses in India had no way to access that capability. That's the gap we fill."
— Latika Sharma, Founder & CEO
